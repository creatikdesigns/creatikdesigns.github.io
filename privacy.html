Privacy Policy

Privacy information for MiniBlue and other Creatik Designs applications.

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Scope: This Privacy Policy applies to MiniBlue – Minimal Blue Icon Pack and other Creatik Designs applications that link to this policy. Individual apps may have additional notices where required.

1. About this policy

Creatik Designs respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use our applications and related services, why it may be processed, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we may process

App and device information

MiniBlue is primarily an offline icon-pack application. Information necessary for Android and compatible launcher features may be processed on your device.

When you use the icon-request feature, the app may process selected app names or package identifiers so that requested icons can be identified. Information submitted through that feature may be sent to the request service configured by the app.

Support or contact information

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, message content and attachments.

Purchases

Where purchases or donations are offered, payment processing is handled through Google Play. Creatik Designs does not receive your full payment-card details through the app.

3. How information is used

We may use information to:

  • provide and operate app features;
  • process icon requests and respond to support messages;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve our applications; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect the service from misuse.

4. Third-party services

MiniBlue may communicate with third-party services needed for specific features, such as the icon-request service and Google Play billing. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

The current icon-request configuration uses the Pacific Manager service at pacificmanager.app.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to deliver a feature you use, when required by law, or when necessary to protect our rights, users or services.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide support, to complete legitimate business needs, or to satisfy legal requirements.

For information you provide directly to Creatik Designs, you may request deletion by emailing creatikdesigns2025@gmail.com. We will review the request and may need to retain certain information when required by law or for legitimate security or accounting purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

Our applications are not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at creatikdesigns2025@gmail.com so we can review the situation.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps, services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
